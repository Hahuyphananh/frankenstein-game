Field notes

How to protect William

Objective

The Creature has escaped into a Victorian village and is making for William Frankenstein. Victor can touch it safely; each touch costs the Creature one of its three lives and sends it back to the start. Catch all three before it reaches William. If it reaches him, William dies and Justine Moritz is later executed after being falsely accused of the murder — Justine is not killed by the Creature. If Victor catches it first, William survives and Justine is never accused.

Difficulty

Easy allows 120 seconds, Normal 90 and Hard 60. Faster catches earn up to three stars, while every setting gives the Creature three lives. The Creature follows the shortest route to William; higher difficulties make it faster.

Current build: one Victorian village story level with William, a goal-seeking Creature, pause, timer and two story outcomes. There is no level progression yet.
